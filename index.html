New conversation every Tuesday

Five generations, one conversation.

Intergenerational Podcast is a bi-weekly show that brings people of different generations into conversation about the future of our planet. Each episode explores the environment, land restoration, climate action, the ocean, agriculture, and the transition to sustainable energy, drawing on the wisdom of elders and the ideas of young people.

The format

Every episode has three parts

Two guests from two generations, one host, and a fixed shape so you always know where you are.

Part one0 to 5 min

The Question(s)

5 min

Each guest bring questions for the other generation. They ask it straightforward, with no preparation from the other side.

Part two5 to 35 min

The Conversation

30 min

The main talk. Plain language, real examples, and room for the awkward questions and the stories that do not fit a headline.

Part three35 to 42 min

The Swap

7 min

Each guest recommends one habit, tool or story from their generation for the other to try, and everything is linked in the show notes.
