The format

Every episode has three parts

Two guests from two generations, one host, and a fixed shape so you always know where you are.

Part one0 to 5 min

The Question

5 min

Each guest brings one question for the other generation. They ask it cold, with no preparation from the other side.

Part two5 to 35 min

The Conversation

30 min

The main talk. Plain language, real examples, and room for the awkward questions and the stories that do not fit a headline.

Part three35 to 42 min

The Swap

7 min

Each guest recommends one habit, tool or story from their generation for the other to try, and everything is linked in the show notes.

Your hosts

Two hosts, twenty years apart

Mira has watched landscapes change for three decades. Dev builds the tools that measure them. They disagree often, and usually politely.
